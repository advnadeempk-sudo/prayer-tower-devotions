# Daily Devotional — Saturday, 14 November 2026

## Prayer for the Nations: My House Shall Be Called of All Nations the House of Prayer

**Scripture:** *"And he taught, saying unto them, Is it not written, My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer?"* — Mark 11:17 (KJV)

**Read:** Mark 11:15–17 · Isaiah 56:6–8 · Psalm 2:8 · Psalm 67 · Matthew 9:35–38

---

Prayer for the nations is not a special ministry for a few missionaries. It is the ordinary heartbeat God intended for His house. When Jesus stood in the temple courts He reached back to the prophets and reminded everyone what the building was for: "My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer." Isaiah had said it first: "mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people" (Isaiah 56:7). God's house was never meant to be a private chapel for one people. It was meant to be the place where every nation is lifted before the throne.

The Greek word behind "nations" is ethnos (plural ethne). It does not mean countries with borders and flags. It means peoples: families bound together by language, culture and story. So when Jesus speaks of a house of prayer for all the ethne, He means every tribe and tongue; the fishermen of a coastal village, the herdsmen of a desert, the office workers of a crowded city, the mountain people no map names. Prayer for the nations is prayer for people, one people group at a time.

Why does God want His house full of this prayer? Because He has promised the nations to His Son. "Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance" (Psalm 2:8). The Father says to the Son, "Ask," and He invites us into that asking. When you pray for the nations you are agreeing with heaven's own agenda. Jesus tied the harvest directly to prayer: "Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest" (Matthew 9:38). Workers go out because someone first knelt down.

Picture a grandmother who cannot travel, has little money and speaks only her mother tongue. Every evening she opens an old atlas, lays her hand on one country and prays for the believers there, for the gospel to be heard, and for peace. She will never meet the people she prays for, but heaven records every name on that page. She is doing exactly what the house of prayer was built for.

You can do the same today. Choose one nation. Pray Psalm 67 over it: "That thy way may be known upon earth, thy saving health among all nations." Ask for labourers, for the Scriptures in every language, for open doors and for peace. Then pray for your own land with the same love.

This is the vision behind the 24-Hour Digital Prayer Tower. Day and night, from every nation, prayer is rising, and the fire on the altar is never allowed to go out (Leviticus 6:13). When you take your hour, you join intercessors across Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas lifting the nations, the persecuted Church, families and the sick before God. The house of prayer for all nations is no longer one building; it is a people awake around the clock.

---

**Prayer**

Lord of the harvest, Your house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations. Make my heart a part of that house today. Give me love for peoples I have never met and faith to ask You for them. Send labourers into every unreached place, open doors that no one can shut, and let Your saving health be known among all nations. Strengthen Your persecuted children with courage and peace. Revive Your Church in my own land and bring a harvest of souls. In the name of Jesus, Amen.

**Pray with us today**

- Persecuted believers in nations where following Christ costs dearly: for courage, daily provision and protection, and that their faithfulness would draw many to the Saviour.
- The unreached peoples of the world: that the Lord of the harvest would send labourers, give them the Scriptures in their own language, and open hearts to the gospel.
- Pakistan and your own nation: for peace in the land, revival in the Church, and a generation of intercessors who keep the fire burning on the altar.
