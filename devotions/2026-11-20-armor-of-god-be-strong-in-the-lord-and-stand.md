# Armor of God: Be Strong in the Lord and Stand Against the Wiles of the Devil

**Daily Devotional — Friday, 20 November 2026**

**Scripture:** *"Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil."* — Ephesians 6:10-11 (KJV)

"Put on the whole armour of God." Many of us have heard the words so often that we no longer stop at them. But the armor of God was written for people under attack, and anyone who prays where faith is costly knows the attack is real. Notice that Paul does not begin with the armour. He begins with the Lord: "be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might." The strength is His before the armour is ours.

The Greek word for "whole armour" is panoplia, from pas, meaning all, and hoplon, meaning weapon. It was the complete kit of the heavily armed foot soldier: helmet, breastplate, belt, shoes, shield and sword. A soldier with a helmet and no shield was not half safe; he was fully exposed in one place, and the enemy only needs one place. That is why the word is whole. Truth without righteousness, faith without the Word, zeal without prayer: every missing piece is the opening the devil looks for. And it is the armour "of God": He forged it, He gives it, and we simply put it on.

In our church we often say that a Christian home is a city with two walls. The outside wall is made of law, locks and watchfulness, and it matters. The inside wall is spiritual: the family altar, the open Bible, a father and mother who pray aloud with their children. A house with only one wall always falls. I have seen families spend everything on the outer wall and leave the inner wall in ruins, and the enemy did not climb over; he walked in through a door nobody was guarding. Proverbs 25:28 says the one who has no rule over his own spirit is "like a city that is broken down, and without walls."

How do we live this today? Put the armour on every morning, piece by piece, in prayer. Name the belt of truth and ask God to make you honest. Name the breastplate of righteousness and confess what needs confessing. Lift the shield of faith and tell the Lord which fiery darts came at you yesterday. Take the sword of the Spirit, the Word of God, and read a passage before you read your messages. Then see how Paul finishes: "praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit." The armour is put on in prayer and kept on by prayer. And do not fight alone; the command is given to a church, not a lone soldier.

This is why the Prayer Tower keeps watch twenty-four hours a day. Somewhere right now a believer is standing where no outer wall protects them at all: no law to appeal to, no neighbour who will speak for them, only the armour of God and the prayers of people they will never meet. When you take your hour, you stand shoulder to shoulder with them, shield touching shield, so that none of us has to stand alone.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, I cannot stand in my own strength, so I come to be strong in You and in the power of Your might. Today I put on the whole armour of God: truth around my waist, Your righteousness over my heart, the gospel of peace on my feet, the shield of faith in my hand, the helmet of salvation on my head, and Your Word as my sword. Expose every unguarded door in my life and in my home, and rebuild the inner wall of prayer. Strengthen my persecuted brothers and sisters where no earthly wall protects them, that they may stand. In Your name, Amen.

- for persecuted believers who face threats and hostility for the name of Jesus, that the whole armour of God would hold and their faith would not fail;
- for the homes of Pakistan and the nations, that the inside wall of family prayer would be rebuilt.
