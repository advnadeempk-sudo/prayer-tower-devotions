# Prayer for Pakistan: Seek the Peace of the City and Pray for It

**Date:** Saturday, 7 November 2026 · **Theme:** Persecuted church & nations

**Scripture:** *"And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captives, and pray unto the LORD for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace."* — Jeremiah 29:7 (KJV)

A prayer for Pakistan is not a political statement; it is an act of obedience. When God's people were carried away to a land that was not their own, where they were few, misunderstood and sometimes mistreated, the Lord did not tell them to withdraw and wait. He told them to seek the peace of the city and to pray unto the LORD for it. And He attached a surprising promise: "in the peace thereof shall ye have peace."

The Hebrew word translated peace is shalom. It means far more than the absence of fighting. Shalom is wholeness: a land where the wells are full, the children are fed, the sick are healed, dealings are honest, neighbours are safe and what is broken is mended. When you pray for the shalom of Pakistan you are asking God for the welfare of every home in it, not only the Christian homes.

Notice the order of the verse: seek, then pray. Prayer for the nation is never an excuse for doing nothing in it; it is the engine of doing good. Build, plant, work honestly, raise your children well, and pray. Notice also the direction: "pray unto the LORD for it." We do not pray to the nation or merely talk about the nation. We carry the nation into the presence of God.

A week ago today our church held the Pakistan Prayer Festival. Thousands came, many from villages, many with very little in their hands. Under a banner that read "Long live Pakistan" they lifted their voices for the land they love. These were people who know what it costs to follow Christ here, and they did not pray for a Pakistan in which only they would be comfortable. They prayed for rain and harvest, for the poor and the sick, for peace in every street. I watched Jeremiah 29:7 come alive: a small and often overlooked people seeking the shalom of the whole city, because God told them to.

So how do you pray for Pakistan today? First, give thanks, because God is at work: house churches are multiplying, children are learning to pray, and clean water wells are opening whole villages to the good news. Second, pray for shalom in plain words: rain in season and fair prices, safe roads and honest dealings, healing for the sick and provision for the poor, and wisdom for all who govern and judge. Third, pray for the Church in Pakistan to be salt and light, holy and brave, generous and quick to forgive. Fourth, pray the Father's cry: that many more would come to know Jesus, the Prince of Peace.

This is why the prayer tower keeps watch twenty-four hours a day. In every hour someone is naming Pakistan before God, and the nations with it. Across the world, persecuted believers are praying the same prayer for the very lands that trouble them, because they too have read Jeremiah 29:7. When you take your hour, you join that unseen company that seeks the peace of the city on its knees.

## Prayer

Lord God, I pray for Pakistan today. Give this land Your shalom: peace in every street, rain in season, bread on every table, healing for the sick and justice for the poor. Give wisdom to all who govern and judge, and let truth and mercy prevail. Make Your Church here salt and light, holy, brave, generous and quick to forgive. Protect my persecuted brothers and sisters and teach us to love those who oppose us. Open the eyes of millions to Jesus, the Prince of Peace. I seek the peace of my city and I pray unto You for it, for in its peace I shall have peace. In Jesus' name, amen.

## Pray with us today

- For Pakistan: that God would give shalom to every province, village and home, send rain and harvest, heal the sick and provide for the poor.
- For persecuted believers in Pakistan and across the nations: courage, protection, and grace to keep praying for those who oppose them.
- For the nations: that the Church in every land would seek the peace of its city and pray unto the LORD for it.
