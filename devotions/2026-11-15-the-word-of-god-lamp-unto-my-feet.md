# Daily Devotional — Sunday, 15 November 2026

## The Word of God: Thy Word Is a Lamp Unto My Feet and a Light Unto My Path

**Scripture:** *"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."* — Psalm 119:105 (KJV)

**Read:** Psalm 119:97–112 · Isaiah 55:10–11 · Matthew 4:1–11 · James 1:22–25 · 2 Timothy 2:8–9

---

The Word of God is what people are really searching for when they look for something solid under their feet. The psalmist found it long ago: "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path" (Psalm 119:105). On this Lord's Day let us remember that the Word of God is not simply a book resting on the pulpit. It is the living voice of our Father, and it has been the fuel of every true revival.

The Hebrew word for "word" here is dabar. It means more than a sound or a sentence. A dabar carries a matter inside it; it is a word that does something. When God speaks, things happen: "So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please" (Isaiah 55:11). The same dabar that called light out of darkness at creation speaks light into your path today.

Notice the picture. It is not a floodlight showing the whole road to the horizon, but a lamp for the feet, enough light for the next step. A traveller in Bible times carried a small clay lamp that lit only the ground just ahead. God rarely shows us the whole journey. He gives us enough for today and asks us to walk by it. Jesus Himself, tempted in the wilderness, answered every attack with "It is written," and the tempter fled (Matthew 4:1-11). The Word that defended the Son of God will defend you.

A newborn baby that refuses milk cannot grow, and a believer who neglects the Word grows weak and open to every fear. I think of a young man, new in the faith, who could barely read. He began with ten minutes a day in the Gospel of John, asking first, "Father, speak to me through Your Word today." Within months the fear that had ruled his nights was gone, and his neighbours were asking why he had changed. Hunger grows by feeding. The more you taste, the more you want (Psalm 119:103).

So live it today. Fix a time and a place. Read a little, slowly. Hide one verse in your heart (Psalm 119:11). Then do what it says, because the hearer who does not obey is like a man who looks in a mirror and forgets his own face, while the doer is blessed in his deed (James 1:22-25). Revival has always begun this way, not with noise but with people who opened the Book and obeyed it.

This is why the Word and prayer belong together in the 24-Hour Digital Prayer Tower. Every hour, somewhere in the world, an intercessor is praying with an open Bible, and many pray in lands where owning that Bible is dangerous. Believers in persecuted places often know whole chapters by heart, because pages can be taken from them but the dabar cannot. "The word of God is not bound" (2 Timothy 2:9). When you take your hour, pray the Scriptures aloud.

---

**Prayer**

Father, Your Word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path. Thank You that when You speak, things happen. Give me a deep hunger for the Scriptures and open my eyes to see wonderful things in Your law. Hide Your Word in my heart so that I will not sin against You, and give me a soft, obedient heart to do whatever You say. Where my path is dark today, light the next step. Strengthen my brothers and sisters who have no Bible of their own, and let Your Word run freely through every nation. Revive Your Church through Your Word, beginning with me. In the name of Jesus, Amen.
