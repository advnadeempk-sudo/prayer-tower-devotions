# Bible Verses About Faith: Now Faith Is the Substance of Things Hoped For

**Daily Devotional — Tuesday, 10 November 2026**

**Scripture:** *"Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen."* — Hebrews 11:1 (KJV)

**Read:** Hebrews 11:1–6 · Mark 2:1–12 · Romans 10:17 · 2 Corinthians 5:7 · Hebrews 11:32–40

---

People search for Bible verses about faith when the ground under them is shaking: a medical report, a court date, a child far from God, a salary that stopped. The Bible answers with one sentence that has steadied believers for two thousand years: "Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen" (Hebrews 11:1). Read it slowly. Faith is not a wish. It is something solid you stand on when nothing visible will hold you.

The Greek word translated "substance" is hypostasis. Literally it means "that which stands under": the foundation beneath a house, the ground beneath your feet. In the everyday Greek of the first century the same word was used for a title deed, the paper that proved a piece of land was yours even if you had never set foot on it. That is what faith is: the title deed of promises you have not yet seen with your eyes. God has spoken; the deed is in your hand; the land is already yours.

Then the writer adds, "the evidence of things not seen." Faith does not deny what you see; it refuses to let what you see have the final word. Verse 6 tells us why: "without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him." Faith holds two convictions at once: God is there, and God rewards those who seek Him.

Faith can also be seen. In Mark 2 four men carried a paralysed friend to Jesus. The house was packed and the door was blocked. They did not turn back. They climbed onto the roof, dug through it and lowered their friend down in front of the Lord. Mark records, "When Jesus saw their faith." Their faith was visible in the shoulders under the mat, in the broken roof, in their refusal to accept any barrier between a hurting man and Jesus. Faith is settled trust in a God who cannot lie, and settled trust always moves the feet.

So what is the roof in front of you today? Find the promise of God that covers your situation, write it down and treat it as a title deed. Then do the next obedient thing: make the call, forgive the person, bring the need to Jesus in prayer. Faith is not pretending the crowd is not there. Faith is finding the roof.

This is why believers pray every hour at the 24-hour prayer tower. Each hour someone carries another person to Jesus on the mat of prayer. Our persecuted brothers and sisters know Hebrews 11 by experience: they hold the title deed of heaven while their homes, their jobs and sometimes their freedom are taken. The same chapter calls them those "of whom the world was not worthy." When you take your hour, you become one of the four friends for someone who cannot reach Jesus alone.
