# Prayer for My Children: Children Are an Heritage of the LORD

**Daily Devotional — Thursday, 19 November 2026**

**Scripture:** *"Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: and the fruit of the womb is his reward."* — Psalm 127:3 (KJV)

Every parent who whispers "Lord, keep my children" knows a particular kind of ache. A prayer for my children is often the most honest prayer I pray, because nothing else so quickly exposes how little I control. Psalm 127 was written for people like us. It opens with the house that the LORD must build and the city that the LORD must keep, and then it turns to the home: "Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD."

The Hebrew word for heritage is nachalah. It is the word used for the portion of land each tribe received in Canaan: not earned, not bought, but allotted by God and held in trust for the family. When the psalmist calls children a nachalah, he says two things at once. Your children are a gift you did not deserve, and they are an entrusted possession that still belongs to the One who gave them. We do not own our children; we steward them for God.

That truth changes how we pray. If my children are mine, prayer becomes anxious bargaining. If they are the LORD's heritage placed in my hands, prayer becomes handing back to Him what was always His, and asking Him to do in them what only He can do.

I think of a father whose son drifted far from the Lord in his teenage years. The father did not lecture him every day; he prayed for him by name every day. He wrote the boy's name in the front of his Bible, and each morning he blessed him aloud, even when the boy was not listening. Years later that young man came home, and the first thing he said was, "I always knew you were praying." Blessing and prayer kept open a door that argument would have slammed shut.

How do we live this today? Pray for your children by name, one at a time, not "the kids" in general. Bless them out loud, the way Jacob laid his hands on his grandsons and spoke good over them. Tell them you love them, and show it. Ask God for their salvation before you ask for their success. And remember Deuteronomy 6: teach the Word as you sit in the house and as you walk by the way, so that the heritage you pass on is not only land or money but living faith.

The Prayer Tower exists because somewhere in the world, at every hour, a parent is awake over a child. In many nations believers are raising children who will pay a price for the faith of their parents, children who are watched at school and mocked for their name. When you take your hour at the tower, you keep watch over someone else's heritage too, asking the Father to guard children you will not meet until heaven.

So lift your children to the LORD today, and lift the children of the persecuted church with them. They are His heritage. He has not forgotten one of them.

## Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You that my children are Your heritage, a gift I did not earn and a trust I cannot keep alone. I hand them back to You by name today. Save them, guard them, and turn their hearts toward You. Give me patience to listen, words to bless, and faith to keep praying when I see no change. Build our home, Lord, for unless You build it we labour in vain. And watch over the children of persecuted believers tonight, that they may stand firm and know they are loved. In the name of Jesus, Amen.
