# Spiritual Warfare Prayer: The Weapons of Our Warfare Are Mighty Through God

**Scripture:** (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;) Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ; (2 Corinthians 10:4-5, KJV)

Spiritual warfare prayer is not shouting at the darkness. It is standing in the light with the weapons God has given and refusing to fight His battles with the world's tools. Paul wrote to a church being pulled apart by proud arguments and clever talk, and he told them plainly: we walk in ordinary bodies, but we do not fight with ordinary weapons.

The word Paul uses for "strong holds" is the Greek ochyroma. It comes from a root meaning "to make firm", and it describes a fortress, a walled place built to hold out against attack. Paul uses it only once in the New Testament, and he aims it at arguments: the "imaginations" and "high things" that raise themselves against the knowledge of God. A stronghold, then, is a lie that has been fortified. It is a thought that has lived in a heart so long that it has walls, a gate and a guard: "God has forgotten me." "My family will never change." "Prayer does not work here." These are the fortresses our warfare is aimed at, and no human weapon can breach them.

I often tell the fathers in our church that every Christian home in a hard place needs two walls. There is an outside wall of lawful protection and wise watchfulness, and there is an inside wall built by prayer, the Word read at the table and a family altar lit every day. A house with only one wall always falls. Many of us have spent years strengthening the outside wall and left the inside wall in ruins, and then we wonder why the enemy walks in through an unguarded heart. Spiritual warfare prayer is the daily work of rebuilding that inside wall, stone by stone.

How do we live it today? First, name the stronghold honestly before God; a fortress you refuse to look at will never be taken. Second, bring the Word against it, because truth is the battering ram that brings lies down. Third, take the thought captive the moment it rises, and hand it to Christ rather than letting it rule your day. Fourth, do not fight alone; ask another believer to stand with you. Our weapons are "mighty through God", which means the power is His, not ours. Our part is simply to pick them up.

This is why the Prayer Tower never sleeps. Somewhere in the world right now a believer is sitting in a cell for the name of Jesus, and the stronghold raised against that brother or sister is not only iron and stone but the lie that they are forgotten. When you take your hour, you are casting down that lie. Intercession is warfare prayer at its purest: we cannot reach the prison, but our prayers go where our feet cannot, and God pulls down what we cannot touch.

Spiritual warfare prayer can feel small. Kneeling beside your bed at midnight does not look like a battle, but heaven counts it as one.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, I come to You in the name that is above every name. I confess that I have tried to fight with my own strength and my own words. Today I take up the weapons You have given. Cast down every stronghold in my heart, every lie that says You have forgotten me, every fear that has built walls against Your truth. Bring every thought of mine captive to Your obedience. Rebuild the inside wall of my home with Your Word and with prayer. Strengthen my brothers and sisters who suffer for Your name, and pull down the strongholds raised against Your church in every nation. The battle is Yours and the victory is Yours. Amen.
