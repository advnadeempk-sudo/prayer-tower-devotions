# Prayer for My Home: Except the LORD Build the House, They Labour in Vain

**Scripture:** Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain. (Psalm 127:1, KJV)

A prayer for my home is one of the most honest prayers a believer can pray. We work for our houses, we worry over them, we save and repair and guard them. Then Psalm 127 opens with a line that quietly turns everything round: "Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it."

The Hebrew word for "house" here is bayith. It can mean a building with walls and a roof, but far more often in Scripture it means the people inside: a household, a family, even the generations still to come. When God promised David a "house", He was not promising bricks; He was promising a family line. So this psalm is not about construction. It is about who is building your family.

The truth is simple: a home is only as strong as the One who builds it. Labour is good; the psalm never tells us to stop working. It tells us that labour without the Lord is "in vain", empty, with nothing lasting to show for it. A father can earn, a mother can plan, parents can guard the door all night, and still a home can crumble from the inside if God is not its foundation.

I think of a family I have known for many years. There was nothing remarkable about their house; the roof leaked when the rains came. But every evening they put their phones aside, shared one meal, and the father asked each child, "What made you happy today? What was difficult?" Then they prayed together, briefly, before bed. When trouble came, and it did come, that home stood, because the Lord had been building it one ordinary evening at a time.

How do we let the Lord build? First, lead by example: children learn far more from what they see repeated than from what they are told. Second, learn the words "I was wrong; please forgive me." A home where parents can say that has a foundation. Third, honour your husband or wife in front of the children, and never humiliate one another. Fourth, keep a short daily rhythm of prayer, thanks and conversation that even the youngest can join. These are small bricks, but the Lord lays them.

The second half of the verse belongs to the prayer tower: "except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain." Across the world tonight, believers are praying for homes they will never see. Some of our brothers and sisters live where simply gathering the family to pray carries a cost, where a believing wife prays alone, or a child keeps his faith quiet at school. When the Lord keeps the city, one praying mother in a hostile town is stronger than any wall, and every hour of the tower, someone is standing watch over such a home.

So pray a prayer for your home today, and then pray for someone else's. Ask the Lord to build what your labour never could.

## Prayer

Lord, build my house. I have worked and worried over my home; now I hand the building to You. Lay Your foundation in my marriage, in my children and in every room of my heart. Teach me to lead by example, to say "I was wrong", and to honour the people You have placed under my roof. Keep watch over us while we sleep and when we are apart. Build the homes of my persecuted brothers and sisters too, so that where there is fear there may be peace. In Jesus' name, Amen.

- For persecuted believers whose homes are watched and whose families pay a price for following Christ, that God would be their watchman.
- For families across Pakistan and the nations strained by poverty, illness or separation, that the Lord would hold them together.
