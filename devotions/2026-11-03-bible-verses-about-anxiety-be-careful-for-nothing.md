# Bible Verses About Anxiety: Be Careful for Nothing and Let God’s Peace Stand Guard

**Date:** Tuesday, 3 November 2026 · **Theme:** Faith & trust

**Scripture:** *"Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus."* — Philippians 4:6-7 (KJV)

Most people who search for Bible verses about anxiety are not looking for a definition. They are looking for a way to sleep tonight. Philippians 4:6-7 is one of the most loved passages in the Bible for that reason. Paul wrote it from prison, chained to a Roman soldier, with a trial ahead and no promise of release.

"Be careful for nothing" is old English for "be anxious about nothing." The Greek word is merimnao, from a root that means to divide or pull apart. Anxiety is a divided mind, a heart torn between tomorrow's fears and today's duties. Jesus used the same word when He told Martha she was "careful and troubled about many things." God is not scolding us here. He is diagnosing us.

The cure is not to try harder to stop worrying. It is to move the worry somewhere. "In every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God." Nothing is too small to mention and nothing is too big to hand over. Prayer is coming to God. Supplication is naming the need plainly. Thanksgiving is remembering what He has already done, which gives faith its backbone.

Then comes the promise. The peace of God "shall keep your hearts and minds." The word for keep is a military one; it means to garrison, to post soldiers on the walls of a city. Paul could see the guard beside him as he wrote. God's peace, he says, will stand guard over your heart the way that soldier stood guard over him. It does not come from understanding everything; it passes understanding.

Think of the four friends in Mark 2 who carried a paralysed man to Jesus. The door was blocked by the crowd, so they dug through the roof and lowered their friend down. "When Jesus saw their faith," He spoke. There is no barrier to a determined faith. Anxiety says the door is blocked and the problem too heavy; faith finds another way to lay the burden at the feet of Jesus. And the man did not get there alone. Some burdens need friends to carry the corners of the mat, so ask your church and your prayer partners to carry you to Jesus. Remove every hurdle between you and Him.

So tonight, when worry knocks, do three things. Name it to God specifically. Thank Him for one thing He has already done. Then leave it with Him and sleep. If the worry returns, pray again; prayer is a doorway you keep walking through until peace stands guard.

This is why a 24-hour prayer tower matters. Anxiety usually visits at night, when no one else is awake. At the tower someone is always praying, so no believer faces three o'clock in the morning alone. Our persecuted brothers and sisters know anxiety of a heavier kind, never sure what a knock at the door may bring, yet many testify that the peace which passes understanding holds. Let us pray that this garrison of peace stands around them tonight.

## Prayer

Father, I bring You every thing that is dividing my heart today. You know the fears I cannot say out loud. I lay them at the feet of Jesus and I thank You for every time You have carried me before. Let Your peace, which passes all understanding, stand guard over my heart and my mind. Quiet my thoughts tonight, strengthen my faith and teach me to pray instead of worry. Keep my family, keep Your church, and surround Your persecuted children with the same peace. In the name of Jesus Christ I pray, amen.
