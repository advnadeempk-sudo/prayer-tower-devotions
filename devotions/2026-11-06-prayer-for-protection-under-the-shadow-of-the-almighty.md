# Prayer for Protection: Under the Shadow of the Almighty

**Date:** Friday, 6 November 2026 · **Theme:** Perseverance & spiritual warfare

**Scripture:** *"He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust."* — Psalm 91:1-2 (KJV)

A prayer for protection is often the first prayer we learn and the last prayer we pray at night. We pray it over children leaving for school, over a husband on the road, over a home in a troubled street. Psalm 91 has been the believer's prayer for protection for three thousand years, and it begins not with danger but with an address: "He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty."

The Hebrew word for secret place is sether. It means a covering, a hiding place, a shelter that cannot be seen into from outside; it is the word for the hollow of a rock where a hunted man hides. God Himself is that hiding place. And the verb for abide is the word for lodging through the night. The psalmist is not describing a quick visit for help when trouble comes. He is describing where you live, and where you sleep.

Notice that the protection follows the dwelling. A shadow only falls on what is near. If I wander far from the Lord and then shout for His shadow in the storm, I am asking for what belongs to those who dwell. Protection in Psalm 91 is not a charm we recite; it is the fruit of a life kept near to God in His Word and in prayer.

I think of a mother in our fellowship who, in a season when fear hung over the whole city, began reading Psalm 91 aloud over her children every morning before they went out and again when they came in at night. Nothing dramatic happened. That was the point. The children stopped flinching at every loud sound, because they had heard their mother say, "He is my refuge and my fortress." The psalm did not remove the danger from the street; it moved the family under a shadow.

So pray for protection the way the psalm teaches. First, dwell: give God your morning before you give it to your phone. Second, speak it: "I will say of the LORD" means you declare His refuge out loud, over your home, your road and your work, as you go out and as you come in. Third, do not trust your own strength, your locks or your cleverness, but the name of the Lord Jesus, who has all authority in heaven and on earth. The shield the psalm promises is His faithfulness, and faith is the hand that holds it.

This is why the prayer tower never closes. Night is when fear is loudest, and someone is always awake there, praying for those who cannot sleep. Remember too the believers who pray Psalm 91 where confessing Christ brings real danger. Some have been kept from the snare; some have gone home to the Lord with this psalm on their lips. Either way they were never outside His shadow. Pray for them, and for the nations where a Christian's only protection is the Almighty.

## Prayer

Lord, You are my refuge and my fortress; my God, in You will I trust. I come into Your secret place today and I bring my family with me. Cover us with Your shadow as we go out and as we come in. Keep us from every hidden snare, every accident and every evil plan. Send Your angels on every road we travel. I do not trust my own strength but the name of Jesus, who has all authority in heaven and on earth. Cover Your persecuted children tonight and be their hiding place where no one else can help. Let us lie down in peace and rise to serve You. In Jesus' name, amen.

## Pray with us today

- For everyone reading this who is afraid for their family, their journey or their home: that they would dwell in the secret place and know the LORD as refuge and fortress.
- For persecuted believers under threat tonight: that God would cover them, confound every hidden snare and give them peace that cannot be taken.
- For Pakistan and the nations: that the Lord would be a shield over His people and that fear would give way to faith in every street.
