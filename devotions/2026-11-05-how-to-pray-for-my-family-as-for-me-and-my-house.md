# How to Pray for My Family: As for Me and My House, We Will Serve the LORD

**Date:** Thursday, 5 November 2026 · **Theme:** Family & marriage

**Scripture:** *"And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."* — Joshua 24:15 (KJV)

If you are asking how to pray for my family, you are already doing the most important thing a parent, husband or wife can do. Joshua, old now, had led armies and divided a land, yet his last public word at Shechem was not about battles. It was about his home: "As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."

The Hebrew word for serve is abad. It is the word for a labourer's work in a field, for a servant's duty to his master, and for the worship of God at His altar; the same word is used when God put Adam in the garden to dress it. Joshua is not describing a Sunday habit. He means that the whole working, eating, quarrelling and forgiving life of his household will be lived under the LORD's rule. And notice the order: "as for me" comes before "and my house." Praying for your family begins with choosing the LORD yourself.

Joshua did not wait for Israel to decide before he decided. A Christian home stands on a covenant, not a contract. In a contract I keep my part only if you keep yours. In a covenant I keep my promise before God whether or not you keep yours. The husband's love and the wife's reverence in Ephesians 5:33 are each one's own responsibility, never a reward for the other's good behaviour.

I think of a couple who came to us with their marriage almost finished. Relatives had taken sides, and the children had gone quiet, the way children do when the shouting starts. We had no clever answers; we prayed with them week after week and helped them speak without blame. Slowly God did what no counsellor can do: He softened two hearts. Today that home is whole, and the children are learning to pray because they watched their parents do it.

So here is how to pray for your family today. Begin with yourself: "Lord, as for me." Confess your own anger, silence or pride before you name anyone else's faults. Pray for your husband or wife by name, blessing rather than correcting. Pray over each child, that they would know the LORD for themselves and be kept from hidden wounds. Pray for the wider family, that interference would turn into intercession. Then set a time, even fifteen minutes at the table with one verse and one prayer, when your whole house serves the LORD together. A praying family becomes a little church in its own living room.

This is why the prayer tower stays open twenty-four hours. Many who come in the night are praying for a cracking marriage or a wandering child. Remember too the believers whose faith has cost them their family: disowned, driven from home, or raising children where confessing Christ is dangerous. For them "as for me and my house" is spoken at great cost. Let us pray for their homes as we pray for our own, and for the nations where a praying family is a light in the street.

## Prayer

Lord, as for me and my house, we will serve You. I bring You my family today. Forgive me where my anger, silence or pride has wounded the people I love. Give me a heart that keeps covenant even when it is hard. Bless my husband or wife; bless each of our children by name and keep them from hidden wounds. Turn every quarrel into forgiveness and every interference into prayer. Make our home a little church where Your Word is read and Your name is praised. Keep the families of Your persecuted children, and be a Father to those cast out for Your name. In Jesus' name, amen.

## Pray with us today

- For every marriage under strain and every parent reading this: that God would heal homes and raise up fathers and mothers who say, "As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."
- For persecuted believers rejected by their own relatives or raising their children in secret: that God would be their Father and keep their households in faith.
- For Pakistan and the nations: that Christian homes would be places of prayer, peace and witness.
