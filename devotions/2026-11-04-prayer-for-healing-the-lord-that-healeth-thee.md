# Prayer for Healing: I Am the LORD That Healeth Thee

**Date:** Wednesday, 4 November 2026 · **Theme:** Healing & comfort

**Scripture:** *"And said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the LORD thy God, and wilt do that which is right in his sight, and wilt give ear to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will put none of these diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians: for I am the LORD that healeth thee."* — Exodus 15:26 (KJV)

If you are searching for a prayer for healing today, you are probably not reading for yourself alone. Someone you love is in a hospital bed, or a report has come back that you did not want. God spoke the words above to a tired, thirsty people at Marah, where the only water was bitter. Before they ever met a physician, they met the Physician: "I am the LORD that healeth thee."

The Hebrew word is rapha. It means to heal, but also to mend, to repair and to make whole again. The Bible uses it for a broken vessel mended, for bitter water made fit to drink and for a ruined land restored. When God names Himself Jehovah Rapha, He is saying: I am the One who takes what is broken and makes it whole. Healing is not only something God does. It is who He is.

Sickness comes for many reasons. Sometimes sin opens the door, as with the paralysed man whose sins Jesus forgave before He said, "Arise." Sometimes the cause is simply medical, like the woman with an issue of blood. Sometimes a spiritual bondage lies behind it, as with the woman bowed together for eighteen years whom Jesus loosed. And sometimes, as with the man born blind, there is no fault at all; God allows it so that His works may be made manifest. Whatever the reason, the Healer is the same.

Look at that woman in Mark 5. She had suffered many things of many physicians, had spent all she had, and "was nothing bettered, but rather grew worse." Yet she said, "If I may touch but his clothes, I shall be whole," and pushed through the crowd. Among a hundred jostling hands Jesus felt one touch of faith, turned, and said, "Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace." She did not have a perfect prayer. She had a desperate faith, and she brought it to Jesus.

So here is how to pray for healing today. Come as you are, with the report, the pain and the fear. Name the sickness plainly; God is not embarrassed by it. Thank Him for doctors and medicine, His gifts, but put your trust in the Giver. Touch Him through His Word, speaking Exodus 15:26 over yourself or your loved one. Then keep coming. Press through discouragement as she pressed through the crowd, one prayer at a time, until you hear Him say, "Go in peace."

This is why the prayer tower never sleeps. Pain does not keep office hours, and a hospital corridor at two in the morning is a lonely place. At the tower someone is always awake to pray for the sick by name. Remember too our persecuted brothers and sisters who fall ill in prison, or cannot safely visit a hospital after being beaten for their faith. They have few doctors, but they have the same Jehovah Rapha. Let us pray His healing over them tonight, and over every nation where sickness and sorrow abound.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, You are Jehovah Rapha, the God who heals. I bring before You every sickness in my body and in my family, and every name that is on my heart today. You know the reports, the pain and the fear. Like that woman in the crowd, I reach out and touch You in faith. Forgive my sins, break every bondage and make me whole. Give wisdom to doctors and nurses, and let Your name be glorified in my healing. Heal Your persecuted children who are sick and wounded for Your sake, and heal our land. I will keep coming to You until I hear You say, Go in peace. In Your mighty name I pray, amen.

## Pray with us today

- For every sick person reading this and every patient in a hospital bed tonight: that Jehovah Rapha would touch them and make them whole in body, mind and spirit.
- For persecuted believers who are ill, injured or imprisoned for their faith and have no doctor to call: that God Himself would be their physician and comfort.
- For Pakistan and the nations: that the sick and hopeless would find the Healer, Jesus Christ, and give glory to God.
