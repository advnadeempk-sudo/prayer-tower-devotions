# Daily Devotional — Monday, 16 November 2026

## Pray Without Ceasing: Keep the Fire on the Altar Burning Day and Night

**Scripture:** *"Rejoice evermore. Pray without ceasing. In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you."* — 1 Thessalonians 5:16–18 (KJV)

**Read:** 1 Thessalonians 5:16–24 · Leviticus 6:8–13 · Romans 1:8–12 · Luke 18:1–8 · Colossians 4:2–4

---

Pray without ceasing. Three small words, and many believers quietly feel condemned by them. How can anyone pray without ceasing with work to do, children to feed and a phone that never stops? Yet the apostle wrote to ordinary working people: "Rejoice evermore. Pray without ceasing. In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you" (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18). This is not a burden for the super-spiritual. It is the will of God for you this Monday.

The Greek word is adialeiptos. It does not mean "non-stop" in the sense of never drawing breath. It means "without gaps that should not be there," like a cough that keeps coming back. Nobody coughs every second, but a persistent cough returns again and again all day. That is the picture: a heart that keeps returning to God, at the stove, on the bus, in the courtroom, at the hospital bed. The same word is used of Paul remembering his friends "without ceasing" in his prayers (Romans 1:9). He did not pray for them every moment; he never let them drop out of his praying.

God gave Israel a picture of this long before. "The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out" (Leviticus 6:13). The priests did not stand at the altar every second, but someone always added wood. Morning and evening the flame was fed, and it never died. Pray without ceasing means tending that fire in your own heart, feeding it often, so it is never allowed to go out.

I think of a mother whose son had walked far from God. She had no hours free for a prayer room. So she tied her prayers to her day: when the kettle boiled, she prayed for him; when she heard the gate, she prayed; before sleep, she lifted his name once more. Years passed. One evening he came home, sat beside her and said, "Mother, I need to come back to Jesus." The fire had never gone out.

So live it today. Choose three hooks in your day, a doorway, a meal, a journey, and let each one turn your heart to the Father. Keep your prayers short and honest; "Lord, help my brother" is a prayer. Carry one name with you all day. Thank Him in the middle of ordinary things, because verse 18 belongs with verse 17. And when you fall silent for hours, do not despair; simply add wood again.

This is the heartbeat of the 24-Hour Digital Prayer Tower. No one person can pray without ceasing, but the Body of Christ can. Across three watches, intercessors in Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas keep the flame alive, so that when you sleep, someone else is praying, and when they sleep, you take your hour. Believers in persecuted lands have long understood this; prayer is not their hobby but their breath, learned while working, walking and waiting. Let us learn from them and keep the fire burning for them.

---

**Prayer**

Father, thank You that You never sleep and never stop listening. Forgive me for the long gaps in my praying and for carrying burdens alone that I should have brought to You. Teach me to pray without ceasing: to turn my heart to You at the stove, on the road, at my desk and in the night, and to give thanks in everything. Keep the fire on the altar of my heart burning; when it grows low, help me add wood again. Strengthen my brothers and sisters who suffer for Your name, and let them know today that they are not forgotten. Make our church a house of prayer for all nations, and use my hour in the tower for Your glory. In the name of Jesus, Amen.
